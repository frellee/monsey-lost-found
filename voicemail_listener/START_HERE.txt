FREE INTERMEDIA VOICEMAIL LISTENER - WINDOWS

This is the NEW free version. Do not use the old Google Cloud/OpenAI API program.

STEP 1: Install Python 3.11 or newer from https://www.python.org/downloads/windows/
Check "Add Python to PATH" if offered during installation.

STEP 2: Gmail needs IMAP access and an App Password.
Open https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords while signed in to the Gmail account receiving Intermedia voicemails.
Google generally requires 2-Step Verification to create App Passwords. If the option is missing, your account or organization may restrict it; some accounts cannot use this method.
Do not turn off account security or share your password. This is a 16-character APP PASSWORD, NOT your regular Gmail password.

STEP 3: Copy .env.example and rename the copy .env (Windows may hide the extension).
Right-click .env and open in Notepad. Replace:
GMAIL_ADDRESS=youraddress@gmail.com
GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD=your_16_character_app_password
with your Gmail address and the new App Password. Save the file.
Do not send your .env or App Password to ChatGPT or anyone else.

STEP 4: Double-click start_windows.bat.
The first run installs free software and downloads a local speech recognition model. It may take several minutes and requires internet access. After that, transcription runs locally on your PC.
Keep the black window open and your PC awake to monitor emails.

STEP 5: Read transcripts.txt in Notepad to see results. A Windows alert appears for possible lost-item messages.

NOTES:
- No Google Cloud project or OpenAI API key needed; no transcription service charges.
- Your Gmail account must allow IMAP access and App Passwords.
- The program reads messages but does not delete or send them.
- The listener checks Intermedia emails every 60 seconds by default.
- Optional .env setting LOOKBACK_DAYS=14 controls how many calendar days back the listener searches. Increase it to cover a longer outage.
- While the listener has no completed voicemails in its database, it selects up to the newest FIRST_RUN_LIMIT emails (default 20) within that window. Once a voicemail is saved, it processes all remaining emails in the window, oldest first.
- The keyword alert is a starter filter and may miss some calls or create false alarms. Transcription of Yiddish/Hebrew may be imperfect.
- Keep your computer on. This is not a cloud service.
- All recordings are downloaded temporarily and deleted after transcription. Transcripts and local credentials stay in this folder; protect it.
- Windows Defender Firewall or antivirus may prompt for permission for Python; use your own security judgment.
- Local model download and Python packages are free, but your internet/electricity costs still apply.
- This version has not been tested against your actual Gmail account.
